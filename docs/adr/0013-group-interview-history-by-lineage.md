# Group interview history by lineage

The interview-history list will represent an original interview and all of its forked descendants as one Interview Lineage instead of showing every Interview Branch as an unrelated row. Branch-specific progress, status, score, and evaluation remain independent and are exposed inside Interview Replay; this keeps alternative answer paths discoverable without flooding history with near-duplicate entries. Its Lineage Summary uses the best completed branch score for display and score sorting, or the most recently active branch's stage and progress when no branch has completed, and never averages scores across related branches.
