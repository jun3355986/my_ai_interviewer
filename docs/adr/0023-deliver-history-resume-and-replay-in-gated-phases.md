# Deliver history resume and replay in gated phases

Delivery will first enable real interview history and reliable tail resume after persistence, migration, state reconstruction, and Turn Processing are verified; branch APIs and scoring semantics follow as a tested backend capability, and the full Interview Replay and fork UI is exposed only after its end-to-end flows pass. This provides early recovery value without presenting controls whose underlying branching, concurrency, or failure guarantees are incomplete.
